## The service

Fantasy League Almanac processes fantasy-league information on the user's
computer and can create a Google Drive spreadsheet in the user's own account.
The current public workflow supports ESPN. Additional platforms may be added
later. The software is not affiliated with or endorsed by ESPN, Google,
Snowflake, Major League Baseball, or any fantasy-sports platform.

The public Google flow requests `drive.file`, creates a new workbook, writes
the almanac, and—only after the user's affirmative disclosure—can set that
workbook to “Anyone with the link — Viewer.” An explicit automation flag may
provide the affirmation non-interactively. Anyone receiving the link can read
the workbook. You control whether to continue, whom to send the link to, and
whether later to change or remove sharing in Google Drive.
