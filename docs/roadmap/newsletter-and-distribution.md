# Newsletter & Distribution

**Linear:** [Newsletter & Distribution](https://linear.app/roguelitedevelopment/project/newsletter-and-distribution-25b8bdf3a28c) · Backlog · Low

An email newsletter edition of the recap, and more broadly the multi-sink
abstraction from the pre-Linear roadmap: BBCode and Sheets are today's two
hardwired consumers; a clean sink interface lets email / Discord / static
HTML plug in without re-threading the consumer scripts. (The old roadmap's
Discord-webhook idea folds in here as just another sink.)

Mostly delivery engineering over content that already exists — cheap
relative to its delight factor. List management for league members is the
only genuinely new concern.

**Depends on:** nothing.

**Seeded issues:** MLB-21 recap → email channel spike (sink interface first)
